# CodeAlpha Data Science Internship Tasks

This repository contains the projects completed during my **Data Science Internship at CodeAlpha**. The internship focused on practical applications of machine learning, data analysis, and statistical modeling using Python.

---

## 🚀 Projects Overview

### Task 1 — Iris Flower Classification
- **Goal:** Classify iris flowers into three species (Setosa, Versicolor, Virginica) based on sepal and petal measurements.
- **Model:** Random Forest Classifier
- **Tools:** Scikit-learn, Pandas, Seaborn, Matplotlib
- **Outcome:** Achieved high accuracy in predicting species based on morphological features.

---

### Task 2 — Unemployment Analysis with Python
- **Goal:** Analyze unemployment rate trends across Indian regions, focusing on the impact of the Covid-19 pandemic.
- **Tools:** Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib, Seaborn
- **Key Insight:** Visualized a significant spike in unemployment rates during the 2020 lockdown period.

---

### Task 3 — Car Price Prediction
- **Goal:** Predict the market price of cars based on features like year, engine power, and mileage.
- **Model:** Linear Regression
- **Tools:** Scikit-learn, Pandas, Matplotlib
- **Outcome:** Successfully predicted car prices with a high R2 score.

---

### Task 4 — Sales Prediction
- **Goal:** Forecast sales revenue based on advertising expenditures across TV, Radio, and Newspaper channels.
- **Model:** Linear Regression
- **Tools:** Scikit-learn, Pandas, Seaborn, Matplotlib
- **Key Insight:** Identified TV advertising as the most influential factor in driving sales.

---
